Brew Haven — Café Logo & Seasonal Ad

Graphic Design2026Completed

Overview

A café logo badge with a pumpkin mark, plus a matching Pumpkin Spice Latte banner ad that carries the same autumn palette and cozy tone.

Problem

A cozy café brand needed a logo and a seasonal promotion that look like they belong together.

Solution

I designed a simple badge logo, then reused its illustration style and palette in a Pumpkin Spice Latte banner ad.

Features

  • Round badge logo with a pumpkin mark
  • Autumn palette of orange, cream, and cinnamon brown
  • Banner ad with the tagline “Every brew is a taste of Haven”
  • Matching illustration style across logo and ad
